  • Click + to open a group. It only opens it.
  • Hover an item to read its details. Nothing else changes.
  • Click a name or a band to show only its route. Each click narrows it and goes on the shelf.
  • Types sit indented under their group. Open a group and its striped bar keeps only the papers that name the group itself. Papers about a type move to that type's row.
  • Each health condition row runs from a bar on the left to a bar on the right. Middle puts the column between compounds and disease pathways: bands come in on the left and leave on the right, so they never cross the names.
  • A health condition filed in several places in MeSH shows up under each one. Its papers are split between them, so nothing is counted twice.
  • Green: a study tested the compound as a treatment.
  • Maroon: a study saw it go up or down with the health condition.
